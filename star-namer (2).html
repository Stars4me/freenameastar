How StarNamer works

This map shows roughly 100 real, bright stars at their true position in the sky (right ascension & declination), plus a scattering of faint background stars for atmosphere. Drag to pan across the sky and scroll (or pinch) to zoom in, just like an old-school map.

Click on any labelled star to see its real astronomical name, distance and constellation. From there you can give it a personal, symbolic name — a dedication for a birthday, anniversary or memorial — and generate a printable keepsake certificate.

Your named stars are saved privately in this browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere). Use the Export button to save a backup file, or Import it later or on another device.

This naming is symbolic and for fun/sentimental purposes only. It does not change the star's official scientific designation — those are governed exclusively by the International Astronomical Union (IAU).
